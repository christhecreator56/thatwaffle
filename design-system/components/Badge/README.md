The scalloped sticker that signs the brand, in four colourways plus the ワッフル seal.

**Provide:** the `badge-waffle`, `badge-umber`, `badge-lily` or `badge-dune` file, or `hanko-waffuru`.

**Use** one badge per scene, at 96px or larger. Match the colourway to the ground: `badge-waffle` on `umber`, `badge-umber` on `justright`, `badge-lily` on `dune`, `badge-dune` on `lily` or `santafe`.

**Don't** put a badge on a photo, redraw its lettering or add text to it. Set offer wording in `OfferTicket`.
