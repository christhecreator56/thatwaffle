A tear-off ticket for one offer: poster, name, discount, validity dates and terms.

**Provide:** every field. An offer missing validity or terms does not publish. The poster is a JPG or PNG under 5 MB.

**Use** one or more on `/offers`, and one on each offer page. After the end date the ticket greys out and the claim action is disabled.

**Don't** ship the sample shown here. It is marked as a sample.
