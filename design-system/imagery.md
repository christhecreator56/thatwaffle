## No cut-outs

Show whole, uncut photos, plate and background included. Cut-out waffles looked off, so the Cutouts group is retired and stays only for reference. When the hero needs a waffle, use the illustration built from four `wedge-900` copies, or a new top-down photo of a whole waffle.

## Spot illustrations

Spots are `spot-chip`, `spot-strawberry`, `spot-blueberry`, `spot-dollop`, `spot-crumbs` and `spot-flying-wedge`.

- Draw flat shapes with three tones per object: base, shade on the right, one highlight stroke.
- Use no outlines and no gradients. This matches the shading in the logo.
- Let them scatter around photos and fall at a different scroll speed. Use three to six in a scene.
- `spot-flying-wedge` replaces the paper plane idea from the poster: the logo's wedge flying with its speed dashes along a dotted path.

## Patterns

- `pattern-seigaiha` sits on `umber` only, at 13 to 16% opacity, at the bottom edge of a scene.
- `pattern-hishi` fills badges and the offer ticket's poster slot. It never sits behind running text.

## Photography for later shoots

Shoot top-down or at 45 degrees on a plain matte surface. Keep every jar, wrapper and packet out of frame. Waffles look best tilted toward camera with the cream or chocolate edge showing.
